## What this establishes

**The ZIP failure is the provider's defect, not our input.** The provider
reproduced it himself at ZIP `60061`, which is the assigned Vernon Hills ZIP,
and found the store present in the UI with the same ZIP. It is escalated to
their R&D. Our own `bad_input` observation of 24 October, recorded in
`BRIGHTDATA_MARIANOS_TEST_2026-10-24.md`, was at ZIP `60601` and is a separate
instance of the same shape.

**Store selection is by ZIP only.** There is no Mariano's store field. For
Vernon Hills that is `60061`. A ZIP does not name a store, so even once this
works, which store a ZIP resolves to must be read back from the output and
checked, not assumed.

**The provider says the fields we are missing are obtainable.** Displayed price
per measure and digital coupon data, for supported stores. Read that exactly as
written: it is "can return ... where available on the page", it is scoped to
supported stores, and Mariano's is not currently a working one. It is a
statement about the dataset's capability, not a delivery, and nothing should be
planned as though those fields are in hand.

## Bearing on the acceptable-use question

This route is the provider's own collection, not our use of Kroger's public API,
so it does not sit under the Acceptable Use of Public APIs question recorded in
`../ACCEPTABLE_USE_POSITION.md`. As with the page-read route, that is a change of
question rather than an answer to it: what the provider is permitted to collect
and resell is between the provider and the retailer, and no claim about it is
made here.
